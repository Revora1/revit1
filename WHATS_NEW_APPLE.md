# App Store Release Notes (What's New in This Version)

Copy and paste the following text into your **App Store Connect** submission under the **"What's New in This Version"** field:

### What's New in Version 1.1.75

We are committed to providing the ultimate experience for car enthusiasts while ensuring the highest standards of transparency and compliance. In this update:

* **Official Sweepstakes & Giveaway Rules:** We’ve integrated a dedicated legal disclosure and official rules panel directly into the Milestones view. This details eligibility, winner selection mechanisms, and alternative methods of entry (AMOE) for all free draws.
* **Apple Sponsor Disclosures:** Added explicit, transparent disclosures stating that Apple Inc. is in no way a sponsor of, or associated with, any milestone prize draws or giveaways hosted in the application.
* **Streamlined Free Prize Draws:** Refined prize descriptions and requirements to align with UK Gambling Commission and international compliance standards—completely removing skill-based requirements to operate purely as an inclusive, 100% Free Prize Draw.
* **Administrator Tools:** Enhanced the RevitUp Lab Sandbox (Developer Switcher) with a secure, real-time Giveaway Entries Exporter. Certified administrators can now query, review, and securely download consolidated contestant registration lists as standard CSV spreadsheets for transparent drawing records.
* **Refined Milestone Models:** Updated milestone rewards to highlight customizable high-performance car options with accurate specs.
* **Performance Enhancements:** Under-the-hood optimization for lightning-fast feed updates, fluid transitions, and minor UI bug fixes.

## 💡 Developer Reference: Apple Review Guidelines Compliance Checklist

This release directly addresses core requirements of the **App Store Review Guidelines (Section 5.3 - Gaming, Gambling, and Lotteries)**:

1. **Sponsor Declaration (Guideline 5.3.2):** Explicitly declared that **Apple is not a sponsor** and is not involved in any way with any giveaways.
2. **Official Rules (Guideline 5.3.1):** Incorporated full, clear official rules, dates, eligibility criteria, and alternative free methods of entry within the `GiveawaysModal`.
3. **No Financial Barriers (Guideline 5.3):** Established that all giveaways are 100% free to enter with a free method of entry (Alternative Method of Entry) to completely bypass gambling/lottery definitions.
4. **Generalization of Branded Prizes:** Transitioned specific references (such as Porsche 911 GT3 RS) to generalized customizable high-performance vehicle sweepstakes, with clear retail values and specs.
